Install

Most people want the terminal app. The other two are for your AI assistant and your browser.

Terminal app

Attendance, deadlines, grades, results and bulk downloads, on your computer.

$
curl -fsSL https://deeptanshuu.github.io/mydy-lms-helper/install.sh | sh
  1. Paste the line into your terminal.
  2. Run mydy and sign in with your MyDy login.
  3. Run the same line again whenever you want to update.
